

## Hide "Edit with Lovable" Badge

### What I'll Do
Remove the "Edit with Lovable" badge from your published site at https://ai-counselor-prep-pulse.lovable.app

### Implementation
Call `set_badge_visibility` with `hide_badge: true` to permanently remove the badge from the bottom-right corner of your published landing page.

### Result
The badge will be hidden immediately on your published site and will remain hidden unless you choose to show it again in the future.
